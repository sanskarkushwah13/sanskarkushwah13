## 🔬 Featured Research

### 📌 Comparative Analysis of GAN Loss Functions in DCGAN-Based Image Synthesis

* **Objective:** Systematic evaluation of major GAN loss functions including Standard GAN, LSGAN, WGAN, WGAN-GP, and Hinge Loss under a controlled DCGAN architecture.
* **Key Findings:**

  * **WGAN-GP** achieves the best image quality (lowest FID scores)
  * **Hinge Loss** provides an optimal balance between stability and computational efficiency
* **Datasets Used:**

  * CIFAR-10 (Natural Images)
  * EuroSAT (Remote Sensing Satellite Imagery)
